PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

Effective September 17, 2026

What the website stores

The storefront uses your browser’s local storage to remember your pickup list, display preferences, and prize-wheel state on that device. That information stays in your browser unless you clear it. The website does not create customer accounts or collect card payments.

Text-message requests

When you choose to send a request, the site prepares a message for your device’s text-messaging app. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself. Your mobile carrier and messaging app handle that message under their own privacy terms.

Analytics and cookies

The site currently has no advertising pixel, third-party analytics, or nonessential cookie tracking. For that reason, it does not display a cookie-consent banner. This policy will be updated before any such tracking is enabled.

Information you send

If you text your name, town, requested products, or notes, that information is used to answer and arrange your pickup or delivery request. Do not send payment-card numbers or other sensitive information.

Questions

For a privacy question or deletion request, text 3233019200.
